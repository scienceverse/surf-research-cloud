Metacheck

PsyArXiv Check: a preprint, checked the way a careful reviewer would

Paste the link to a preprint on PsyArXiv and receive a checklist of what to fix and what to improve, with every automated check metacheck has: statistics, p-values, effect sizes, power analyses, ethics statements, preregistrations, references, and the data and code the paper links to. The record on PsyArXiv is checked too: whether the open data can be reached, the license, ORCID iDs and the conflict of interest statement. Everything runs on SURF Research Cloud in the Netherlands.

Check a PsyArXiv Preprint

Paste a link to the preprint, its DOI, or its id.

For example https://next.psyarxiv.org/preprint/28j7s, https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/28j7s or 10.31234/osf.io/28j7s.

What happens

Your browser reads the preprint's record from PsyArXiv. The metacheck server then fetches the PDF from PsyArXiv, checks it, and keeps the report for one hour; a report can only be fetched with its random job number, which only your browser knows. Preprints on PsyArXiv are public. With the Crossref box ticked, the paper's reference list is sent to Crossref (USA). Automated checks miss things and flag things that are fine: read every finding as a question, not a verdict. Other checks: papers and repositories, ResearchBox, the TU/e data manager check.

Built on Metacheck, open source software by scienceverse, and the PsyArXiv API.
